omlette:

Incredients:

  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 small piece of butter (about 1/2 tablespoon)
  • A pinch of salt and black pepper
  • chopped veggies

steps to be followed:

  1. take a frying pan and heat it in high flame
  2. drop the butter on it and let it slide all over the pan
  3. crack the 2 eggs in an seperate vessel in cylindrical shape (for convenience)
  4. spread the pepper and salt over the egg along with the veggies
  5. stir it well
  6. pour it over the heated pan and spread it
  7. turn it over to make both sides cook
  8. serve it in a plate and enjoy
