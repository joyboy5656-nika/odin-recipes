coffee:

Incredients:

NOTE: the measurements mentioned are for 1 person

  • coffee powder(1 tsp)
  • milk(100ml)
  • sugar(2 tsp)

steps to be followed:

  1. take a container and pour the milk in to it
  2. leave it alone after turning the flame high till it boils
  3. in the mean time take a cup and put the coffee powder and sugar in to it
  4. after the milk boils pour it into the cup and mix it well
  5. take a couple of biscuits and enjoy your break time
