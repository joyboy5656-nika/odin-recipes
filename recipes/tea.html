coffee:

Incredients:

NOTE: the measurements mentioned are for 1 person

  • tea powder(2 tsp)
  • milk(50ml)
  • sugar(2 tsp)
  • water (50ml)

steps to be followed:

  1. take a container and pour the milk in to it
  2. leave it alone after turning the flame high till it boils
  3. in the mean time take another container and pour the water into it
  4. let it boil too and contine by adding tea powder and sugar afterwards
  5. add ginger if you want a strong tea
  6. filter the solution and add it with the milk which would have boiled by now
  7. serve it in an cup and enjoy and i recommend to drink it bfr it cools down
